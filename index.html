How Should You Spend Your Money to Be Happier?
A 2023 reanalysis by Kahneman, Killingsworth and Mellers of 33,391 employed adults found happiness keeps rising with income for most people, and levels off beyond about $100,000 only among roughly the unhappiest fifth. Separate research finds spending on experiences tends to bring more happiness than buying things, but mainly for people with money to spare. The author advises covering essentials first when money is tight, then seeking shared experiences you can afford.

How to Get People to Open Up: Lessons From AI
A study of 492 participants (Kleinert et al., 2026) found people felt closer to an AI partner than a human after deep-talk chats, but only when the AI was labeled as human.
